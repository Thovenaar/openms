## Fixed-tick runtime

`new OfflineField(scene,store,hooks)` requires a prepared scene with `scene.simulation`, `scene.actor`, `manifest.life`, and `manifest.combat`. It attaches itself to `scene.offlineField` and exposes:

- `await prepare(signal)`: acquire original mob presentation only for the initial viewport plus half-viewport margin; failed/cancelled candidates release owned resources.
- `step(30,input)`: exactly one gameplay update per executed original 30-ms physics quantum. A non-30-ms step is an error. There is no second accumulator and no RAF gameplay timer.
- `action`, `playback`, `blocksMovement`, `dead`: avatar presentation and next-tick input-lock projection. Locks retain physics gravity/inertia. Attacks must pass through one idle/base-action tick before a new attack can start, so the idempotent animation API cannot hide a repeated same-action restart.
- `updateDemand()`: scene demand scheduler calls this outside simulation; at most two template loads may be pending.
- `snapshot()`: policy, current player/mob statistics, exact rectangle state, action/time, special restrictions, residency, and explicit unsupported capabilities.
- `destroy()`: idempotent. Removes display membership before destroying entities/subtextures, aborts pending work, and releases leases. Async completion cannot resurrect a destroyed owner.

Hooks observe accepted events, not predictions: kills, strikes, effects, profile changes, ordinary attacks, mob/player hits, HP recovery and player death. Kill/death notifications occur only at the lethal transition; reactor strikes occur once per committed impact. Field-owned `CombatPresentation` consumes hit/recovery outcomes; the persistent audio owner plays authored available cues. `onPlayerDeath()` clears active skill buffs and opens the ordinary revival confirmation through the UI/Main map-transition owner.

Tick order is original player physics (Main), original ordinary player receiver, mob timer/motion/frame/body updates, player input/action/impact/recovery, type-0 mob attack decisions/impacts, body contact damage, signed hit-timer/tint update, movement-lock projection, and artwork seeking. All current gameplay state is independent of Pixi residency. `EntityAnimation.seek(mob.actionMs)` projects already-decided original artwork timing; RAF must exclude `gameplayOwned` entities.

## Recovered ordinary player hit response

- `tryReceiveHit({amount,direction,locallyInitiated,source?,attackAction?})` is the shared outcome boundary for contact and authored attacks. Locally initiated hits reject a nonzero signed timer or dead player; already-authorized outcomes bypass those two guards. An ordinary attack pose does **not** confer incoming-hit immunity. Optional `attackAction` is accepted-impact provenance for audio, not damage authorization; contact clears it rather than borrowing a pending attack pose. Unsupported original status/deadline fields are not mapped to arbitrary local attack state.
- `009591fb..00959227` installs **+1500 ms for positive damage**, **−1500 ms otherwise**. Both signs block local admission. Each original 30-ms avatar update approaches zero by 30 before choosing tint; a newly admitted hit is therefore observed as 1470 ms at that quantum's draw.
- `00930b27` increments a persistent counter while the positive timer is active and the player is alive. Two of each four updates use RGB **`0x808080`**, the other two **`0xffffff`**. This is color modulation, not disappearance or half alpha. Nonpositive outcomes protect without blinking; expiry, death and recovery restore normal tint.
- Ordinary direction selects **(−200,−200)** or **(+200,−200)** pixels/s; the original `0x7fffffff` sentinel selects a zero vector. Offline contact derives the direction from geometry outside the impulse primitive. `007a6353` detaches foothold contact and enters air, clears grounded velocity first, preserves stronger aligned airborne velocity, and combines opposing components rather than blindly overwriting them. The browser's separate ladder reference is detached consistently.
- The old 300-ms movement-locking hit phase is removed. An admitted positive hit selects the actor's original `hit` facial expression for1500ms; zero/negative outcomes do not start that expression. This distinct emotion deadline is not locomotion stun. Existing frame alpha remains intact; tint is applied on the actor parent and covers resident and subsequently selected parts. [Expression/carry instructions](ghidra-client-corrections/fidelity-r2-emotion-carry.txt) retain the original emotion branch.
- Damage magnitude, autonomous mob decisions and progression remain explicitly local. Ordinary passive recovery now follows the recovered producer below; temporary-status and special bonus states remain unmapped.

The outer producer is now traced through `009f5c50 → 009f84d0 → 00531b8d → 00a03350 → 009717b5 → 0094a144 → 00930b27`: one counter increment per30ms update, not per render frame. Thus each two-tick gray/normal phase is **60ms**, independent of the signed1500ms protection interval. The separate5000ms alert deadline is not invulnerability. [Motion recovery](ghidra-client-corrections/motion-recovery.json) retains the exact exports. The reported excessively fast appearance remains a discrepancy to compare with an original recording, not grounds for an invented slower timer.

## Recovered natural recovery

`PassiveRecovery` is owned by `OfflineField` and executes after the current physics/contact outcome, once per30ms quantum. [Producer/eligibility](ghidra-client-corrections/regen-consumer.txt), [instructions](ghidra-client-corrections/regen-insns.txt), and [formulas](ghidra-client-corrections/regen-formulas.txt) establish independent ordinary timers: HP10 and MP3 every10000ms, multiplied by the map's original `info/recovery` (absent ordinary multiplier1) and truncated. There is no fabricated “three seconds since damage” delay or sitting +5/+3.

HP requires alive, below maximum, unchanged X/Y and an admitted standing/sitting action; movement or failed eligibility resets HP progress. MP requires alive and below its own maximum but continues through movement and actions; failed MP eligibility resets only its timer. Timers reset on firing, with no catch-up burst. HP presentation receives the producer amount, while durable HP/MP clamp to their maxima.

Learned-only hooks `skillLevel(id)` and `skillInfo(id,rank)` supply actual durable ranks and WZ level fields; job never grants ranks. Warrior-family learned1000000 gives5000ms standing HP and its WZ`hp` bonus. Magician2000000 MP bonus is `trunc(characterLevel * learnedRank * 0.1)`. `hooks.onRecovery(amount,simulation)` forwards HP only to `CombatPresentation.onRecovery`. Revival is a separate UI/map transition, not a natural-recovery timer.

## Explicit local rules

- One stable record is created for every authored mob placement. Authored hidden/limited/inactive entries are explicitly inactive. Missing positive maxHP or missing original action artwork is classified inactive rather than assigned synthetic values/artwork. Flying/special/missing-foothold movement is stationary, not fake ground AI. Selected-skill-only OctoPirates remain immune to the basic sword.
- Ordinary ground mobs initialize against their exact authored foothold, projecting authored x onto its finite original segment. They patrol only contiguous positive-x floor links within the authored `rx0..rx1`, turning at ranges, gaps, walls, unsupported links and plane/group changes. The original segment supplies y. A traversal has a 32-link bound; exhaustion records an explicit fault. No random AI, jump permission or flying trajectory is invented.
- Local horizontal speed is `60 * max(0,100 + original speed) / 100` pixels/s. Only the input normalization by 100 is original evidence; the conversion to 60 pixels/s and patrol rule are local. Ground admission requires original `move` artwork, an exact positive-x foothold and ordered finite range, with no positively identified flying controller. Nonzero `mobType` is not a stationary flag. `noFlip` locks artwork/body/attack mirroring, not movement direction.
- A new attack edge starts equipped `swingO1`, or `proneStab` while crouched, only if the profile actually equips weapon 1302000 and original action timing/rectangle are present. No attacking while climbing. A held button does not repeat. Basic attacks consume zero MP. Impact is the first tick at or after half the original avatar action duration.
- One nearest eligible overlapping original current mob body is selected, with authored record order breaking equal-distance ties. Damage is `max(1,floor((4*profile.str+profile.dex)*equippedWeapon.incPAD/10 - target.PDDamage/2))`, capped at remaining target HP. STR/DEX come from the durable local profile; starter weapon `incPAD=17` and target defense come from original WZ (the original loader's absent PDDamage default is zero). The STR multiplier, division by 10, and defense division by 2 are **provisional local choices**, not a recovered original damage formula. The retained outgoing consumers establish geometry, not a safe authoritative damage equation. At bootstrap STR12/DEX5 the rule yields 90 damage before defense, 30 against Hector's PDDamage120; enemy HP is not normalized into a fixed hit count, and progressing STR/DEX increases damage.
- Nonlethal damage plays original `hit1` once if available and pushes an ordinary ground mob at 120 pixels/s for 240 ms, subject to the same original floor/range boundaries. Missing hit/die actions do not fabricate new frames. At zero HP the mob cannot contact, attack or be targeted; `die1` holds its final frame and disappears after its original duration. Respawn is after at least 10 seconds at the resolved original spawn, not an interpretation of `mobTime=-1/0/1800`. Respawn resets previous/current positions together to prevent false sweeps.
- Mob HP/MP initialize from original maxima. Every local five seconds, authored `hpRecovery/mpRecovery` values are applied up to maxima. Supported type-0 attack selection rotates through original attack records after a local 2400-ms cooldown, requires overlap and sufficient original `conMP`, consumes that cost, then applies the exact rectangle at the original `attackAfter`. Unknown types/rectangles/timing never generate fake attacks.
- Original `bodyAttack=1` enables local contact damage. Incoming magnitude is `max(1,ceil(original PADamage/20))`, or MADamage for a selected original magic attack; this equation remains local policy. Missing damage stats disable that outcome explicitly. Admission and ordinary hit reaction use the recovered contract above, replacing the old guessed 1200/300-ms and 140/−150 values.
- Supported self-stat buffs add cached PAD to the attack input and reduce the local incoming expression by `PDD/2` or `MDD/2` before ceiling/minimum-one damage. These are integrations into the declared local equations, not recovered server equations.
- At zero HP attack/locomotion are locked and the ordinary original revival confirmation opens. Jump does not revive and no guessed three-second local full-restore path remains. The confirmed transition follows the authored return map as described below.
- Killing awards original WZ `exp` once, then `awardExperience(profile,amount,hpGrowth)` applies the shared local threshold `15*level*level`, level cap200. Each level gives local base +5 maxHP plus the supported learned HP-growth consumer's bonus, +3 maxMP, +1 STR/DEX/INT/LUK, and full HP/MP. The helper also accepts a quest transaction draft and does not persist itself; the caller marks the shared profile dirty and emits effects. Learned growth is not an original EXP/base-stat table, and job selection never grants a learned rank. No automatic AP/SP, job advancement, inventory drop, meso reward or loot table is invented.

## Dynamic resource ownership

`packageMap` keeps mob pixels in the map texture/atlas closure but excludes mob entities from static streamed regions. `manifest.life.renderables[template]` contains `{entity,atlases,bounds}` using original frames/parts and normal atlas descriptors. Descriptor bounds are **presentation demand extents only**, never hitboxes. Every template is represented once, not once per mob. Main also preserves `scene.combat` and `scene.reactors` in the map manifest.

`OfflineMobRenderer` owns `VisualTextures` per demanded template and calls scene `addDynamicEntity/removeDynamicEntity`; the scene owns display membership, not those textures. A viewport leaving an authored static cell cannot reset mob position/HP/death/timers. Far-away artwork is removable while the full authored mob simulation continues; re-entering demand constructs original artwork and seeks directly to the existing simulation phase. The normal atlas CPU/GPU budgets and global scene sprite/entity ceilings still apply. Failed loads retain explicit scene/renderer errors rather than claiming residency. No per-tick fetch, load scheduling, allocation of actor/rectangle state, sorting, or resource mutation is performed.
